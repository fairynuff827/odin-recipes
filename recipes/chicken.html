Spanish Chicken and Rice Recipe

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 60minutes | Servings: 6

I detest a long story that I have to scroll through to get to a recipe, so I won't torture you with that.

Here it is:

Ingredients:

    • 6-8 chicken thighs or drumsticks (whatever can comfortably fit in your pan)
      1 1/2 cups medium grain rice washed
      1tbs olive oil
      1 red bell pepper, chopped
      1 yellow onion, chopped
      2 cloves garlic, peeled and crushed
      1 can crushed tomatoes
      3tbs tomato paste
      3 cups chicken broth

    For Spice Rub

      1tbs smoked paprika
      garlic powder
      1tsp salt
      1tsp black pepper
      1/2tsp cayenne pepper

    • Instructions:

    • 1. Pre-heat oven to 375 degrees.
      2. Wash rice, let soak for 15min while preparing the rest.
      3. Mix spice rub ingredients together in a small bowl.
      4. Coat the chicken with the rub, make sure to get it under the skins as well.
      5. In a deep cast iron skillet, add the olive oil and brown the chicken for a few minutes on each side. When all chicken pieces are browned, remove from the pan and set aside (they're still raw on the inside).
      6. Add the chopped veggies and crushed garlic to the pan and sauté for 5min, stirring occasionally.
      7. Add the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, drained rice, and chicken broth to the pan and mix. Then place the chicken pieces back on top.
      8. Place the entire cast iron pot in the oven and bake at 375 for 45min.
      9. When done, take the chicken back out and give the rice and veggies a good stir. Let sit 10min for the rest of the broth to absorb.
      10. You can shred the chicken off the bone and mix into the rice, or leave whole and serve each piece with a scoop of the rice and veggies.
