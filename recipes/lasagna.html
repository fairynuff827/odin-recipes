Lasagna Recipe

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 40minutes | Servings: 2

I detest a long story that I have to scroll through to get to a recipe, so I won't torture you with that.

Here it is:

Ingredients:

    • 12 lasagna noodles
      1 pound ground beef
      1 jar (24 oz) spaghetti sauce
      1/2 cup water
      1 egg
      1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
      2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
      1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
      1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

    • Instructions:

    • 1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
      2. Cook lasagna noodles as directed on package. Drain and rinse with cold water.
      3. Brown ground beef in a large skillet; drain. Stir in spaghetti sauce and water.
      4. In a medium bowl, beat egg. Stir in ricotta cheese, 1 1/2 cups mozzarella cheese, Parmesan cheese and parsley.
      5. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, spread 1/3 of meat sauce. Layer with 4 lasagna noodles, 1/2 of cheese mixture, and 1/3 of meat sauce. Repeat layers with remaining ingredients, ending with meat sauce on top.
      6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
      7. Uncover and sprinkle with remaining 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese.
      8. Bake uncovered for an additional 25 minutes or until cheese is melted and bubbly. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
