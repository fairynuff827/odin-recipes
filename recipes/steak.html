Filet Mignon Recipe

Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Servings: 2

I detest a long story that I have to scroll through to get to a recipe, so I won't torture you with that.

Here it is:

Ingredients:

  • Filet Mignon Steaks:

      2 (8oz) filet mignon steaks
      1tsp kosher salt
      1/2tsp black pepper
      2tbs high heat cooking oil (e.g.: avocado oil)

  • Basting Butter:

      2tbs butter
      2 sprigs rosemary
      2 garlic cloves

    • Instructions:

    • 1. Pre-heat oven to 400 degrees. Remove the steaks from the fridge and let come to room temperature.
      2. Season all sides of the steaks with salt and pepper.
      3. Heat cast iron pan on stove, on high heat.
      4. Sear the steaks in the hot cast iron pan for 2-3 minutes on each side, until a golden brown crust forms.
      5. Roll them on their sides for another 1 minute to sear the edges.
      6. Flip each steak, and add the butter, garlic, and rosemary to the pan. Baste the steaks with the melted butter for 1-2 minutes.
      7. Transfer the pan to the preheated oven and cook for 4-10 minutes, depending on desired doneness.
      8. Remove the steaks from the oven and let them rest for 5 minutes before serving.
